# NAVARIS Sector Management Matrix

## Cross-sector management layer
Business Management; Financial Management; Project Management; Operations; Commercial/Sales; Supply Chain; Quality; Digital Transformation; AI/Automation; Risk; Training.

## Sector applications
Energy/Oil & Gas: well intervention, slickline/wireline, maintenance, field services, equipment/supplies. Maritime/Ports/Logistics: port services, stevedoring, shipping, freight forwarding, supply chain. Aviation/Air Cargo: cargo operations and logistics. Construction/Infrastructure: project management, cost, procurement, execution. Mining/Extraction: operations, supply and management. SMEs/General Management: productivity, cash flow, strategy and transformation.

## Rule
General management methods are reused across sectors; sector-specific technical claims require sector evidence or qualified partners.
