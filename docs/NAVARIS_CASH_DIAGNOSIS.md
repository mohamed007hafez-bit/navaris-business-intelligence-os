## Funnel equations
Let L = contacted leads, q = qualified-lead rate, o = offer rate among qualified leads, w = win rate among offers, CM = contribution per won engagement, and FC = fixed cost for the same period.

Expected contribution (simplified) = L × q × o × w × CM.
Operating profit = realized engagement contribution − FC.
Cash collected is tracked separately from booked revenue and invoiced receivables.

Without verified funnel inputs, do not fabricate expected cash.
