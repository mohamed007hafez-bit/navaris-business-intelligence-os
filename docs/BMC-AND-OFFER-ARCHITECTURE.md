# NAVARIS Business Model and Offer Architecture

## Core problem
Organizations and suppliers often have capabilities or problems but lack a qualified route from need to solution, partner, execution and cash.

## UVP
Turn Business Problems & Market Opportunities into Qualified Commercial Action.

## Customer segments
OEMs; suppliers; contractors; SMEs; logistics/maritime companies; energy/Oil & Gas; infrastructure; construction; aviation/air cargo; mining/extraction; investors; institutions.

## Entry offers
1. Business Health Check. 2. Operational Performance Assessment. 3. ISO 9001:2026 Readiness Assessment. 4. Financial Diagnostic. 5. Feasibility Study. 6. Market/Tender/RFQ Intelligence.

## Transformation offers
Management-system implementation support; operational improvement; digital transformation/AI/automation; strategic management; training.

## Execution model
NAVARIS coordinates communication, strategy, commercial development, contracts/signing where authorized, supervision and monitoring. Specialized execution can be delivered by qualified teams or partners.

## Revenue paths
Paid Diagnostic -> Project Fee -> Retainer -> Representation Fee -> Success/Commission where appropriate -> Training -> Implementation.

## Primary KPI
CASH COLLECTED.

## Funnel KPIs
Qualified Opportunities; Conversion %; Gross Margin; Days-to-Cash; Pipeline Value; Diagnostic-to-Contract %; Partner-to-RFQ %; Repeat Revenue.
