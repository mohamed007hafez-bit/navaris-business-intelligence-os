# NAVIS Freight Forwarder — Founder Meeting Brief & Lean Canvas
**Version:** 1.0 | **Date:** 10 October 2026 (Africa/Cairo, UTC+3) | **Prepared by:** NOVA | NAVARIS Business Operations & Development
**Status:** Working hypothesis; not legal, tax, insurance, investment or chartering advice.

## 1. Executive decision
Do not start by renting an office, buying software, vessels, trucks or containers. Start as an asset-light freight coordination/forwarding service with vetted, licensed carriers, customs brokers and shipping agents. Sell a narrow service to a verified shipper with a real shipment; collect a clearly agreed service fee; measure whether the customer repeats.

**First offer to test:** “We compare and coordinate qualified freight options, document requirements and shipment milestones for Egyptian SMEs exporting/importing through Alexandria and nearby industrial zones, with transparent charges and exception escalation.”

## 2. Dissertation-to-business link
The published research is “SocioEconomic Challenges In The Shipping Sector: The Impact Of Digital Transformation On The Work Of Freight Forwarders,” SocioEconomic Challenges 9(4), pp. 99–116 (2025), DOI 10.61093/sec.9(4).99-116.2025. The study used 393 employees in freight-forwarding firms in Alexandria and SPSS/AMOS. It examined technology adoption, process automation and digital integration; time efficiency, cost efficiency and service quality; organizational readiness as a partial mediator; and governmental support as a moderator of the digital transformation/time-efficiency relationship.

**Practical translation:** use a shared shipment tracker and document checklist; log every milestone and exception; standardize quotations and SOPs; automate repetitive status updates only after the process is stable; train the team; integrate customer, broker, carrier, port, invoice and payment status. Compare baseline versus pilot using quote turnaround, document error rate, milestone visibility, on-time delivery, cost per shipment, gross profit per shipment, claims, DSO and cash conversion. These are testable hypotheses, not guaranteed results.

User reports >500 views and >90 downloads; do not publish those numbers as independently verified unless the publisher's live counter is checked and captured.

## 3. A/B business analysis
### A — Analysis → Equation → Evaluation
- **Problem:** SMEs may struggle with fragmented quotes, document errors, low shipment visibility, delays, and unclear total logistics cost. Treat these as hypotheses until confirmed by interviews or shipment evidence.
- **Equations:** Gross profit per shipment = customer service revenue − direct purchased transport/handling/documentation costs. Quote turnaround = complete quote timestamp − complete shipment-request timestamp. On-time milestone rate = milestones achieved on/before agreed time ÷ milestones due. DSO = average trade receivables ÷ credit sales × days in period. Cash contribution = customer cash received − cash outflows directly attributable to the shipment.
- **Evaluation:** continue only if a real buyer shares a shipment brief, the service can be performed legally and operationally, expected gross margin is positive, payment terms are financeable, and risk is contractually controlled.

### B — Business model → Buyer validation → Build/operations → Bookkeeping/cash → Better/scale
1. Interview 10 relevant importers/exporters; ask for their last shipment problem, current provider, decision criteria, volume/frequency, route, documents, service failures and willingness to pay.
2. Obtain one complete real shipment brief and two comparable quotes from vetted providers.
3. Deliver a bounded paid pilot through authorized counterparties.
4. Record invoice, cash received, direct costs, gross profit, claims and payment delay.
5. Repeat only after proof of service quality and positive contribution.

## 5. Initial customer/database strategy
**Official discovery source:** Egypt Export Portal company directory: https://www.expoegypt.gov.eg/exporters . Portal registration/services: https://www.expoegypt.gov.eg/ . Import/export registration context: GOEIC https://www.goeic.gov.eg/en/pages/default/view/52 . Use public company-level records to discover accounts, not to infer freight demand. A directory listing is not proof that the company currently needs a forwarder.

**Required fields:** Lead_ID; legal/company name; buyer/supplier/forwarder/carrier/customs broker/port category; sector; city/country; official website/source URL; published business email/phone; source date; export/import evidence; likely lane (hypothesis); cargo/shipment type; recurring-need evidence; publicly sourced decision-maker/role; ability-to-pay evidence; verification status; fit score and scoring basis; last contact; next action; owner; consent/opt-out; quote status; agreement status; expected revenue (scenario only); actual cash collected.

**Priority segments:** (1) Alexandria/Borg El Arab/Amreya/Beheira manufacturers and exporters with repeat shipments; (2) SMEs needing a second quote, shipment coordination or document/milestone visibility; (3) Egypt↔Gulf/Europe routes after confirming route, product, Incoterms, timing, restrictions and provider capacity; (4) oil cargo/tanker chartering only as a separate high-risk lane, not the first low-capital pilot.

Before representing the entity as a shipping agent, confirm licensing, company registration, tax/VAT, port access, insurance, contractual liability and professional requirements with relevant authorities and qualified local counsel/accountant. A partner's customs-clearance license does not automatically license the new entity for other activities.

## 8. Founder meeting checklist
Do not leave with only a verbal “we will partner.” Record:
1. Legal name and intended activity of the proposed entity.
2. Who contributes cash, expertise, customer access, systems and working time.
3. Equity/ownership and decision rights; do not assume equal split.
4. Roles/RACI: customer acquisition, quotations, documentation, clearance, transport, invoicing, collection and claims.
5. Which partner licenses cover which activities and what must be separately licensed.
6. Customer ownership, confidentiality, non-circumvention and data access.
7. Pricing, transparent commission/fee, direct costs, margin split and who bears FX/claims risk.
8. Customer funds: who receives them, reconciliation, credit limits, payment-before-booking rules.
9. Carrier/broker agreements, insurance, liability caps and complaint/claims process.
10. Exit, dispute resolution and no-poaching clauses reviewed by counsel.
11. 7-day pilot: one target segment, 10 interviews, one qualified shipment brief, two comparable quotes, one written offer.
12. No public claims, contracts, purchases, or binding commitments until Human Gate and legal checks are complete.

## 9. 48-hour action plan
**Day 1 (10 Oct):** conduct founder meeting; complete checklist; agree target customer and role boundaries; start official-source company discovery; create lead database with source/date/evidence status. No mass outreach based only on directory entries.
**Day 2 (11 Oct):** verify 20–30 candidate company records as a target, not a guaranteed output; shortlist 5–10 high-fit accounts only where source and shipment profile support the fit; make 3–5 personalized approaches after human approval; request real shipment briefs; qualify one pilot.
**By day 7:** produce one paid pilot offer with two comparable provider quotes if available; document price, service scope, direct costs, payment terms, liability and collection plan.

## 10. Risk controls
- Never claim all European oil inventories are low or every buyer needs a tanker; verify current market and company-specific demand.
- Tanker/oil cargo work requires verified mandate, vessel/owner, ISM/P&I, cargo provenance, sanctions/AML screening, charter-party expertise, credit/LC/escrow review and specialist legal advice.
- Do not invent company contacts, budgets, freight rates, commissions, conversion rates or expected cash.
- Record actual cash separately from forecasts. CASH COLLECTED remains zero until payment evidence exists.
- Do not publish the 500+ views / 90+ downloads as publisher-verified unless captured live; user reports these counts.

## 11. Naming
