# NAVARIS Methodology Architecture v1.0
**Status:** DRAFT — methodology architecture only; not yet validated on a live client engagement.  
**Owner:** NAVARIS Business Operations & Development  
**Control rule:** FACT → SOURCE → CALCULATION → ASSUMPTION → INTERPRETATION → RISK → ACTION.  
**Commercial control:** No invented rates; no external commitments without approval; cash collected = 0 until documentary proof.

## 1. Design principle
NAVARIS will use a source-backed, original operating method informed by publicly available professional guidance and standards. This is not a claim of affiliation with McKinsey, BCG, Deloitte, ISO, IIA, or Kirkpatrick Partners, and does not reproduce any firm's proprietary internal methodology or templates.

Each deliverable must show: purpose, scope, owner, inputs, method, evidence, calculations (where applicable), assumptions, limitations, review checklist, approval gate, version/date, and source register.

## 2. Service methodology map

### A. Management Consulting — structured problem solving + value realization
1. Frame the decision and define the problem; agree objective, scope, constraints and baseline.
2. Build a hypothesis tree / issue tree and prioritize the questions that can change the decision.
3. Create a workplan and evidence plan; assign owners and data sources.
4. Validate data quality; distinguish FACT, INFERENCE, ASSUMPTION and UNKNOWN.
5. Analyze the relevant drivers (financial, operational, customer, organization, market).
6. Quantify value where inputs support it; label scenarios and sensitivity; do not fabricate missing values.
7. Develop options, trade-offs, risks, implementation requirements and recommendation.
8. Translate into a 30/60/90-day or otherwise agreed action plan with accountable owners and KPIs.
9. Track benefits against a baseline; separate forecast value from realized value.
10. Close with client approval, evidence index, lessons learned and receivables status.

**Core templates:** Client Intake & Qualification; Problem Definition; Issue Tree & Hypothesis Log; Data Request & Evidence Register; Diagnostic Workbook; Financial/Operational Analysis; Options & Decision Matrix; Business Case & Sensitivity; 30/60/90-Day Action Plan; Executive Report; Benefits Realization Tracker; Proposal/SOW.

**Core checklists:** Client Qualification; Problem Framing; Data Integrity; Calculation QA; Hypothesis/Evidence Validation; Recommendation Feasibility; Implementation Readiness; Report QA; Closure & Collection.

### B. Internal / QMS Auditing — risk-based, criteria-led, evidence-based assurance
Use ISO 19011 guidance for management-system audits. For internal audit functions, use the IIA Global Internal Audit Standards where applicable. For ISO 9001 QMS audits, define the audit criteria from the applicable, verified edition of ISO 9001 and the organization's own documented processes.

1. Define audit purpose, scope, criteria, boundaries and independence/conflicts.
2. Understand the process and risk context; establish audit programme / plan.
3. Determine sample strategy and evidence needs; protect confidentiality.
4. Conduct interviews, observation, record review and sampling.
5. Record objective evidence and trace each finding to a stated criterion.
6. Classify findings only according to the agreed audit procedure and evidence.
7. Communicate conclusions, limitations and any unresolved evidence gaps.
8. Track correction/corrective action and verify implementation/effectiveness.
9. Close only when required verification evidence is documented.

**Core templates:** Audit Programme; Risk Universe / Risk Assessment; Audit Plan; Criteria & Clause Matrix; Process Audit Checklist; Sampling Plan; Interview/Evidence Log; Finding/NCR Record; Corrective Action & Root Cause; Verification Log; Audit Report; Follow-up Register.

**Core checklists:** Independence & Conflict; Audit Readiness; Criteria Coverage; Evidence Sufficiency/Traceability; Sampling; Finding Wording; Report QA; Corrective Action Verification; Closure.

**Guardrail:** A consultancy gap assessment is not automatically an independent audit. Do not claim certification or third-party certification authority unless properly authorized.

### C. Training — needs analysis + backward design + transfer and outcome evaluation
Use ISO 10015 guidance for competence management and people development. Use the Kirkpatrick Model as one evaluation framework, planning from desired results backwards to learning design.

1. Define business result / performance need and sponsor expectations.
2. Identify target learners, current capability, required capability and work-environment barriers.
3. Write measurable learning objectives linked to job tasks.
4. Design curriculum, practice, materials, assessments and facilitator plan.
5. Confirm readiness: logistics, accessibility, materials and learner data handling.
6. Deliver and capture attendance plus formative feedback.
7. Assess learning using suitable tests, simulations, role-play or work samples.
8. Plan workplace application and manager support.
9. Evaluate behavior transfer and organizational results when data and follow-up access permit.
10. Report evidence, limitations, improvement actions and commercial closure.

**Core templates:** Training Needs Analysis; Course Proposal; Learning Outcomes Map; Curriculum/Session Plan; Facilitator Guide; Learner Workbook; Pre/Post Assessment; Attendance Register; Reaction/Learning Evaluation; Transfer Plan; Impact Report; Training Closure & Collection.

**Core checklists:** Needs Validation; Objective/Assessment Alignment; Material QA; Delivery Readiness; Facilitation Quality; Learning Evidence; Workplace Transfer; Impact Measurement; Closure & Collection.

## 3. Universal evidence and quality controls
- Every material factual claim has a source, owner, date and link/reference.
- Every calculation shows formula, units, input values, source and caveat.
- Missing information is marked UNKNOWN / NOT PROVIDED / NOT CALCULABLE.
- Forecast, target, estimate and realized result are never conflated.
- Each recommendation has a rationale, owner, deadline, KPI, risk and next action.
- Each template has a unique ID, version, owner, review date and status: DRAFT / REVIEWED / APPROVED / RETIRED.
- Client-facing use requires a human review gate; no automatic external sending.
- Cash collected is recorded only when bank receipt or other documentary evidence is present.
