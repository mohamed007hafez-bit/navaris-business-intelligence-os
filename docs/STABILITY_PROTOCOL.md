# NAVARIS STABILITY PROTOCOL — CASH-FIRST v1

## Purpose
Stop research drift and convert NAVARIS into a controlled, evidence-gated commercial operating system whose only primary outcome is **CASH COLLECTED**.

## The single business lane
For the stabilization period, NAVARIS prioritizes:
**VERIFIED DEMAND → QUALIFIED SUPPLIER → NAVARIS COMMERCIAL ROLE → AGREEMENT → OFFER/RFQ → DEAL → CASH → REPEAT**

Primary sectors:
- Oil & Gas wells / drilling / intervention
- Refineries / process plants
- Ships / maritime maintenance
- Offshore platforms
- Energy / industrial maintenance

Initial product families:
- Fishing Tools
- Wellhead
- BOP
- API 610 Pumps
- LPG Hoses 6" and 8" × 12m
- Closely related maintenance items only when supported by demand evidence

## Non-negotiable anti-distraction rules
1. No broad market research without a defined commercial question.
2. No supplier search before a real or strongly evidenced demand is recorded, except a short supplier benchmark required to test supply feasibility.
3. No opportunity becomes ACTIVE without a source-backed demand signal.
4. No cash number is presented as FACT unless commercially evidenced.
5. Expected Cash = scenario only unless fee/commission/margin and transaction value are documented.
6. Days-to-Cash = assumption unless a real process/timeline is documented.
7. No external outreach, quotation, tender submission, purchase, representation commitment or contract without Human Gate.
8. Research that does not improve a Gate, Offer, Deal or Cash action is DEFERRED.
9. One active priority at a time; maximum three supporting tasks.
10. Every run ends with **GO / HOLD / KILL** and exactly one next commercial action.

## Evidence gates
**G0 SOURCE:** official/primary source or clearly identified secondary source.
**G1 DEMAND:** identifiable buyer/counterpart and evidenced need.
**G2 REQUIREMENT:** item/specification/code/quantity/date captured where available.
**G3 SUPPLY:** qualified OEM or Tier-2 supplier with verifiable capability/certification evidence.
**G4 FIT:** technical + commercial + geographic fit.
**G5 AGREEMENT:** NAVARIS role and commercial terms are agreed before commitment.
**G6 ACTION:** Human Gate passed and next action is executable.
**G7 CASH:** invoice/payment/commission receipt is evidenced.

Failure at G0-G2 = KILL or HOLD, do not expand research.
Failure at G3-G5 = HOLD, identify the missing proof.
G7 is the only state that increases **CASH COLLECTED**.

## Source hierarchy
1. Official buyer / government / tender portal
2. Official company procurement/vendor portal or document
3. Official OEM/manufacturer documentation
4. Recognized certification/standards body
5. Reputable secondary source only for discovery, never silently upgraded to FACT

## Mandatory opportunity record
Opportunity ID | Demand | Buyer | Country | Asset | Item | Code/Spec | Source | Supplier | Supplier Evidence | NAVARIS Role | Agreement Status | Commercial Model | Expected Cash | Days-to-Cash | Gate | Next Action | Status

## 5-stage supplier QC
1. Identity / legal entity / manufacturer status
2. Technical specification conformity
3. Certificate / API / ISO verification as applicable
4. Manufacturing / inspection / traceability evidence
5. Pre-shipment/FAT + final documentation
Product-specific tests are only added when required by the applicable standard/specification.

## Cash discipline
Use:
- Expected Transaction Value = documented or quoted value
- NAVARIS Fee/Commission = documented rate or explicit assumption
- Expected Cash = Transaction Value × NAVARIS Fee/Commission × Probability, only as a scenario
- Actual Cash Collected = cash actually received

Never back-solve or invent a transaction value to make an opportunity look attractive.

## Daily operating loop
1. Review existing active opportunities first.
2. Verify new demand.
3. Advance the highest Gate-ready opportunity.
4. Search supply only for qualified demand.
5. Prepare one commercial action.
6. Human Gate.
7. Record outcome.
8. Archive evidence.
9. Repeat.

## Stability KPI
Primary: **CASH COLLECTED**
Secondary: verified demands, qualified suppliers, offers submitted after Human Gate, deals won, days-to-cash, repeat rate.

## Definition of success
Not more rows. Success is:
**Paid Result → Cash Collected → Evidence → Repeatable Route.**
