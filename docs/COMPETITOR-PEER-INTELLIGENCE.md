# 🧭 COMPETITOR / PEER INTELLIGENCE

## Purpose

This module establishes the intelligence architecture for NAVARIS before collecting or comparing external companies.

The system must answer two different questions separately:
1. What should NAVARIS become and execute?
2. Who exists on the demand and supply sides, what do they offer, and where can NAVARIS create a legitimate commercial connection?

External intelligence must never be allowed to redefine NAVARIS's identity without explicit human review.

## NAVARIS's Middle-Layer Function

NAVARIS should create value through five controlled activities:
1. **Origination** — find or receive the real need/opportunity.
2. **Qualification** — verify need, buyer, timing, fit, economics and evidence.
3. **Matching** — identify capable and relevant supply/partners.
4. **Orchestration** — structure communication, commercial route and execution responsibilities.
5. **Conversion to Cash** — move the opportunity through proposal, contract, delivery and collection.

## Intelligence Rules
- Never manufacture competitor data.
- Never infer private relationships from weak signals.
- Separate **fact**, **source**, **analysis**, and **hypothesis**.
- Record source date because market positioning changes.
- Do not use competitor intelligence to make unsupported claims of superiority.
- A competitor can simultaneously be a potential partner, subcontractor, supplier, or benchmark.
- External intelligence informs NAVARIS decisions; it does not replace human commercial judgment.

# 4. COMMERCIAL INTELLIGENCE FLOW

**Demand → Evidence → Qualification → NAVARIS → Supply/Partner → Commercial Route → Execution → Collection**

The intelligence system should continuously improve this loop:

**Observe → Record → Validate → Compare → Identify White Space → Test Commercially → Measure Cash → Learn**
