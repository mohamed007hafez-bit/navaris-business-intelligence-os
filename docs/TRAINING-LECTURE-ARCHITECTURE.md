# NAVARIS Training Architecture — Petroleum, Maritime & Logistics
## 3-Hour Applied Lecture Design Standard

Status: DESIGN BASELINE
Purpose: Convert verified source books/materials into commercially useful, decision-oriented executive training.

## NAVARIS training philosophy

Training is not a chapter-by-chapter summary. Every lecture must move:

CONCEPT → INDUSTRY CONTEXT → EVIDENCE → ANALYSIS → KPI/FORMULA → CASE → SIMULATION → DECISION → IMPROVEMENT → ACTION

Primary outcome:
The participant should be able to diagnose a real operating/commercial problem, quantify its impact where data permits, make a defensible decision, and define an improvement action.

## Target audiences

- Oil & Gas managers and supervisors
- Operations and maintenance leaders
- Engineers moving into management
- Shipping-company managers
- Ship agents / port agents
- Freight-forwarding managers
- Logistics and supply-chain professionals
- Commercial / procurement / customer-service teams
- Business owners and executives

## 3-hour lecture clock

0:00–0:15 | Executive opening
- Why this topic matters
- Business problem
- Learning objectives
- One real-world decision question

0:15–0:45 | Concepts & terminology
- Definitions
- Process / operating model
- Industry-specific context
- Source-book concepts

0:45–1:15 | Analytical framework
- Drivers
- KPIs
- Financial/operational relationships
- Data required
- FACT vs ASSUMPTION

1:15–1:25 | Break

1:25–2:00 | Industry case
- Company/project scenario
- Evidence pack
- Root-cause analysis
- Bottleneck / risk / opportunity identification

2:00–2:30 | Simulation & decision
- Scenario A / Base / Stress
- KPI and financial impact
- Alternatives
- Decision matrix
- GO / VERIFY / HOLD / NO-GO

2:30–2:50 | Improvement design
- Corrective action
- Preventive action
- Process redesign
- Responsibility
- KPI target
- 30/60/90-day implementation

2:50–3:00 | Executive report
- One-page management report
- Decision
- Key risk
- Next action
- Assignment / field application

## Standard lecture deliverable

Each lecture must generate:
1. Instructor deck
2. Instructor notes
3. Participant handout
4. Case/evidence pack
5. KPI/formula sheet
6. Simulation exercise
7. Decision worksheet
8. Improvement action plan
9. One-page executive report template
10. Source map linking every substantive concept to the source material

## Source integrity

- Do not invent book content.
- When the five source materials are provided, map each lecture to exact chapter/section/page references.
- Distinguish SOURCE FACT / INDUSTRY PRACTICE / NAVARIS ANALYSIS / ASSUMPTION.
- Use external/current industry facts only when independently sourced.
- Do not reproduce copyrighted books; transform concepts into original teaching material and cite sources.

# TRACK A — OIL & GAS

## OGC-01 Operations Management
Modules:
- Oil & Gas operating system
- Production/service workflow
- Maintenance and reliability
- Downtime and utilization
- Productivity
- HSE interface
- Bottleneck analysis
- KPI architecture
- Operational cost drivers
- Continuous improvement

Decision case:
A field/service operation has declining utilization and rising emergency work. Diagnose the loss, quantify the operational/cash impact, and choose the improvement priority.

Core KPIs:
Availability, utilization, downtime, MTBF, MTTR, planned vs unplanned maintenance, productivity, cost/well, cost/hour, HSE indicators.

## OGC-02 Project Management & Project Controls
Modules:
- Project lifecycle
- Scope/schedule/cost
- WBS
- Critical path
- Progress measurement
- Earned-value concepts
- Delay analysis
- Change control
- Risk
- Recovery planning

Decision case:
A petroleum project is late and over budget. Build a recovery plan and decide whether to rebaseline, accelerate, resequence, or escalate.

## OGC-03 Economics, Finance & Commercial Decision-Making
Modules:
- Revenue/cost structure
- CAPEX/OPEX
- Contribution margin
- Break-even
- Cash conversion
- Working capital
- Project economics
- NPV/IRR/WACC
- Pricing
- Contract economics
- Commercial risk

Decision case:
Compare two service opportunities with different prices, margins, payment terms and working-capital requirements. Select the one that maximizes risk-adjusted cash.

## OGC-04 Procurement, Supply Chain & Contract Management
Modules:
- Procurement strategy
- Sourcing
- Supplier qualification
- Technical/commercial evaluation
- Lead time
- Inventory
- Logistics
- Contract structures
- Payment terms
- Claims/change orders
- Supplier performance

Decision case:
A critical well-service component is unavailable locally. Build a sourcing route, qualify the supplier, assess landed cost and lead time, and determine the commercially viable procurement model.

## OGC-05 Leadership, Strategy & Performance Management
Modules:
- Strategic diagnosis
- Competitive position
- Operating model
- Leadership
- Organization/capability
- KPI cascade
- Performance management
- Digital transformation
- AI/ERP opportunities
- Execution governance

Decision case:
A mid-sized oilfield company has revenue but weak profitability and inconsistent execution. Decide whether to optimize operations, restructure, reposition, digitize, or pursue a combination.

# TRACK B — MARITIME / SHIPPING / FREIGHT FORWARDING / AGENCY

NAVARIS should treat maritime logistics as one operating ecosystem rather than isolated job titles:

SHIP OWNER / OPERATOR ↔ SHIPPING LINE ↔ PORT ↔ AGENT ↔ FREIGHT FORWARDER ↔ CUSTOMS / TRUCKING / WAREHOUSE ↔ CARGO OWNER

## M-01 Maritime Transport & Shipping Operations
- Maritime ecosystem
- Vessel voyage cycle
- Port-call lifecycle
- ETA/ETD
- Berthing
- Cargo operations
- Documentation
- Safety/compliance interface
- Delays and port costs
- Demurrage/detention concepts
- Operational KPIs

Decision:
Choose actions to reduce port-call delay and protect service reliability.

## M-02 Ship Agency & Port Agency Management
- Role of ship agent
- Pre-arrival planning
- Port formalities
- Husbandry
- Crew/cargo coordination
- Port service providers
- Documentation
- Disbursement-account logic
- Agency revenue/cost structure
- Principal communication
- Exception management

Decision:
A vessel arrival is disrupted by documentation, berth and service-provider issues. Build the agency action plan and quantify commercial consequences.

- Tracking
- Claims
- Customer service
- Pricing and margin

Compare routing alternatives and select the solution using total landed cost, transit time, reliability and margin.

## M-04 Shipping Lines, Commercial Management & Network Economics

- Freight pricing

## M-05 Maritime Logistics Commercial Development & Agency Growth

Build a 90-day commercial plan for a shipping/logistics company or foreign maritime supplier entering Egypt/MENA.

# NAVARIS INSTRUCTOR METHOD

For every lecture, the instructor should repeatedly ask:

1. What is the business problem?
2. What evidence do we have?
3. What KPI proves the problem?
4. What is the financial/cash consequence?
5. What are the root causes?
6. What alternatives exist?
7. What happens under stress?
8. What decision should management make?
9. What must change operationally?
10. Who owns the action?
11. How will success be measured?
12. When will the improvement produce cash or measurable value?

## Final lecture output

The participant leaves with:

PROBLEM
→ EVIDENCE
→ ANALYSIS
→ OPTIONS
→ SIMULATION
→ DECISION
→ IMPROVEMENT PLAN

→ OWNER

This is the NAVARIS difference: training is designed as a management decision laboratory, not a conventional academic lecture.
