## Unit economics
Planning inputs supplied:
- General engagement contribution margin (CM): USD 150.
- Petroleum engagement CM: USD 500.
- Fixed costs (FC): EGP 0–5,000.

Formulas:
- CM = Revenue − variable cost.
- Operating profit: π = n × CM − FC.
- Break-even volume: n_BE = FC / CM, only when FC and CM share currency and time period.

The supplied FC is in EGP and CM in USD. A valid break-even calculation therefore requires an approved exchange-rate assumption and consistent period definitions. Do not treat n_BE = 1 as universally valid.
