# NAVARIS Governance

## Human Gate
Required before external messages, tender/RFQ submission, contracts, purchases, investments, binding partner commitments and public claims.

## Evidence policy
Material external facts require a source. Current/recent facts require a dated source. Unknown is preferable to invented.

## Calculation policy
Deterministic formulas belong in the formula registry. Inputs, units, currencies, periods and assumptions are recorded.

## AI policy
Agents may research, classify, calculate, summarize, draft and flag. They do not autonomously create binding commitments.

## Versioning
Material changes to formulas, prompts, schemas or workflows require a change note and regression test.

## Privacy
Keep the repository private. Sensitive client data should not be placed into public repositories. Use minimum necessary data.

## Audit trail
Record source, calculation, assumption, reviewer, decision and outcome whenever material.

## Conflict control
If sources conflict, preserve both, identify the conflict, prefer the stronger/primary source and do not silently reconcile unsupported differences.
