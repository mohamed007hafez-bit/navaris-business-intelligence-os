## 5. Company intelligence — market structure
Public company pages show that the Egyptian market includes specialist Slickline/Wireline providers and integrated oilfield service firms. Several public profiles describe direct service execution, equipment, crews, or partnerships. This supports the existence of a potential partner ecosystem, but does not establish a shortage, unmet demand, market size, pricing, margins, or NAVARIS's right to represent any provider.

**Strategic implication:** NAVARIS must qualify as an independent coordinator only where the customer has a real coordination need and an appropriately qualified executing partner is willing to contract for execution. Avoid presenting NAVARIS as a field service provider.
