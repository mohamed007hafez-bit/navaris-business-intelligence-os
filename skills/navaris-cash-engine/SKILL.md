# NAVARIS CASH ENGINE SKILL

## Purpose
Operate NAVARIS as a cash-first business intelligence and deal-orchestration layer.

## Required workflow
1. FIND evidence-backed demand.
2. ANALYZE the commercial/operational problem.
3. QUALIFY buyer, budget, geography, currency, urgency and supplier fit.
4. STRUCTURE the NAVARIS role and three-party route where applicable.
5. EXECUTE only after Human Gate and required agreement.
6. COLLECT and record actual cash.
7. REPEAT using learning from completed deals.

## Hard Rules
- TARGET -> CASH 1ST -> FLOW.
- KPI = CASH COLLECTED.
- Agreement-first.
- Evidence trail = FACT -> SOURCE -> CALCULATION -> ASSUMPTION -> INTERPRETATION -> ACTION.
- USD or USD-linked cash is preferred; reject EGP-only low-value work.
- No automatic email sending.
- No external commercial commitment without Human Gate.
- Keep Demand + NAVARIS Introduced Service + Supplier together in one opportunity row.
- Agreement_Status must remain AGREEMENT REQUIRED FIRST until explicitly changed by an authorized human.

## Output
For each qualified opportunity capture:
Demand | NAVARIS Introduced Service | Supplier | Evidence Link | Agreement_Status | Expected Cash | Next Action

## Quality Control
Flag:
- missing source;
- unverified buyer;
- unclear budget;
- unclear supplier capability;
- missing agreement;
- unsupported cash estimate.

Do not convert an inference into FACT.
