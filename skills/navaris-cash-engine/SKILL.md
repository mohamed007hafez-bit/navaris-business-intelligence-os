# NAVARIS CASH ENGINE SKILL

## Purpose
Operate NAVARIS as a cash-first, evidence-gated business intelligence and deal-orchestration layer. The engine exists to produce qualified commercial action and cash, not research volume.

## Single operating lane
VERIFIED DEMAND -> QUALIFIED SUPPLIER -> NAVARIS ROLE -> AGREEMENT -> OFFER/RFQ -> DEAL -> CASH -> REPEAT

## Required workflow
1. FIND only evidence-backed demand or a specific missing Gate.
2. ANALYZE the requirement and commercial problem.
3. QUALIFY buyer, timing, value/budget, geography, currency, urgency and supplier fit.
4. STRUCTURE the NAVARIS role and three-party route where applicable.
5. EXECUTE only after Human Gate and required agreement.
6. COLLECT and record actual cash.
7. REPEAT using evidence from completed deals.

## Hard anti-distraction rules
- TARGET -> CASH 1ST -> FLOW.
- KPI = CASH COLLECTED.
- One active priority at a time; maximum three supporting tasks.
- No broad research without a defined Gate or commercial action.
- Do not search suppliers broadly before demand is qualified.
- No unsupported cash estimate.
- Unknown/VERIFY is preferable to invention.
- Agreement-first.
- No automatic external commitment.
- Research without a next commercial action is DEFERRED.

## Evidence gates
G0 SOURCE -> G1 DEMAND -> G2 REQUIREMENT -> G3 SUPPLY -> G4 FIT -> G5 AGREEMENT -> G6 ACTION -> G7 CASH.

A failed G0-G2 is KILL/HOLD; do not expand research.
A failed G3-G5 is HOLD with a precise missing-proof action.
Only G7 changes CASH COLLECTED.

## Evidence trail
FACT -> SOURCE -> CALCULATION -> ASSUMPTION -> INTERPRETATION -> RISK -> ACTION

## Supplier QC
1. Identity/legal/manufacturer status
2. Technical/spec conformity
3. Certificate/API/ISO verification as applicable
4. Manufacturing/inspection/traceability
5. Pre-shipment/FAT + final dossier

## Output
For each active opportunity:
Demand | Buyer | Requirement | Supplier | Supplier Evidence | NAVARIS Role | Agreement_Status | Gate | Expected Cash (scenario) | Actual Cash | Next Action

## Human Gate
Required before external messages, tender/RFQ submission, quotation, purchase, representation commitment, contracts, investments or other binding commitments.

## Quality Control
Flag missing source, unverified buyer, incomplete requirement, unclear budget/value, supplier capability gap, missing agreement, unsupported cash, stale source, conflicting evidence and duplicate opportunities.

## Strategic Master AI — mandatory methodology
When a strategy, crisis, expansion, company or tender is assessed, follow `docs/STRATEGIC_MASTER_AI_STANDARD.md` in order:
1. Environmental & positioning: PESTEL, Porter's Five Forces, weighted IFE/EFE, QSPM, then Blue Ocean/value curve if the IFE governance gate allows.
2. Alignment & execution: strategy map, Balanced Scorecard, OKRs, daily KPIs; OEE only for suitable asset/manufacturing processes.
3. Financial/crisis: DuPont and CCC when relevant data exists; Altman Z-score only with a model appropriate to company type and complete financial inputs.
4. Growth: Ansoff, NPV/IRR and incremental vs radical innovation, including working capital, tender costs, payment terms and crisis-risk premium.
Mandatory outputs: numerical matrix with equations and sources; if inputs are missing mark NOT CALCULABLE and list them; cash-linked crisis diagnosis; next-morning OKR action plan; open methodology links; opportunity-by-opportunity risk and next action.
Apply user governance triggers: IFE <2.5 means defense/internal repair before expansion approval; a valid applicable Altman score <1.1 triggers liquidity defense. Label these as NAVARIS governance triggers, not universal academic thresholds. Report conventional model interpretation as well. Positive NPV and IRR above cost of capital are necessary but not sufficient for a tender or expansion decision.
Never invent ratings, financial values, cash flow, source evidence or company health. Separate actual cash received from forecast cash. Always include date/time/timezone and report title.
