# NAVARIS Hybrid Deal-to-Cash & Oilfield Logistics Skill v1.0
Status: Active operating specification; not proof that a background agent is continuously running.
Owner: NAVARIS Business Operations & Development
Priority: CASH ENGINE first; Business Intelligence OS second.
Operating geography: Alexandria / Borg El Arab / El Max / Wardian; Red Sea / Gulf of Suez oilfield locations; Suez / Adabiya / Ain Sokhna; expand only against verified demand.
Model: zero-capital intermediary; buyer → NAVARIS (commercial introduction / coordination) → qualified supplier and licensed logistics providers.

## 1. Non-negotiable controls
- No inventory, purchase advances, freight advances, customs advances, guarantees, financing, or unapproved spend by NAVARIS.
- No external email, WhatsApp, tender inquiry, bid, or disclosure of a supplier identity until the user approves the specific recipient and final message. Drafting and research may proceed.
- Never claim a tender is a buyer request; a project, port, company, news article, or supplier catalogue is only a signal until a named, authorized buyer need is evidenced.
- Never invent a freight rate, shipment, quantity, savings percentage, profit, commission, delivery time, supplier authorization, certification, or customer relationship.
- No commission is protected until a written agreement defines trigger, base, percentage/amount, payment timing, non-circumvention, scope, governing law, and parties. NCNDA alone is not a substitute for a lawful, enforceable fee agreement.
- Do not seek preferential treatment, share confidential tender information, or bypass procurement controls. Apply sanctions, export controls, customs, safety, dangerous-goods, anti-bribery, and tender-integrity checks.
- CASH COLLECTED stays zero unless payment evidence is reviewed and logged.

## 2. Seven gates — all required before QUALIFIED
1. SOURCE_VERIFIED: primary/official source or authenticated buyer request, URL/date/reference captured.
2. BUYER_DEMAND_VERIFIED: named buyer/authorized contact, item/service, quantity, location, needed-by date and procurement route.
3. SUPPLIER_AND_CARRIER_VERIFIED: technical fit, written availability/quote, route capacity, licenses, insurance, safety credentials and relevant references.
4. ZERO_CAPITAL_VERIFIED: no NAVARIS-funded goods, freight, customs, taxes, bond, credit, guarantee or working capital.
5. FEE_AND_NONCIRCUMVENTION_SIGNED: lawful written fee/commission agreement signed before introduction or work begins.
6. COMPLIANCE_CHECKED: procurement eligibility, tender contact rules, sanctions/export/import/customs, product classification, dangerous goods, HSE and conflict checks.
7. ECONOMICS_VALIDATED: itemized landed cost / logistics cost, currency and validity, payment milestones, commission base, net fee, delay risk, tax and downside case.

State labels: NEW → SIGNAL_ONLY → BUYER_DEMAND_PENDING → PARTNER_QUALIFICATION_PENDING → COMMERCIAL_TERMS_PENDING → QUALIFIED_STRUCTURED_DEAL → INVOICE_PENDING → CASH_COLLECTED_VERIFIED. Use BLOCKED, EXPIRED, or REJECTED where appropriate. Never skip a state.

## 4. Evidence hierarchy
Tier 1: official tender/RFQ, buyer procurement portal, signed request, direct authorized buyer confirmation.
Tier 2: official company project / supplier portal / port authority source.
Tier 3: supplier quote, carrier quote, license, insurance, technical datasheet and references (validate authenticity).
Tier 4: reputable news and industry directories as lead-generation only.
Tier 5: Meta/WhatsApp forward, AI-generated table, unreferenced price or claimed saving = unverified until independently substantiated.
Store FACT, SOURCE, DATE, INFERENCE, UNKNOWN, NEXT ACTION separately.

## 5. Cash-first score (0–100; do not score missing facts as positives)
- Buyer demand and procurement authority: 25
- Supplier/carrier ready with written quote: 20
- Fee agreement accepted before introduction: 20
- Zero-capital structure: 15
- Net fee and payment timing: 10
- Compliance / delivery feasibility: 10
Missing evidence receives zero for that subscore. Any failed mandatory gate blocks qualification regardless of score. Do not rank by headline contract value alone.

## 6. Financial and logistics calculations
- Landed cost = product price + origin handling + freight + insurance + port/terminal charges + customs/duties/taxes + inland delivery + inspection/permit costs + other evidenced charges.
- NAVARIS net fee = contractually earned gross fee − direct NAVARIS costs − taxes/withholding − payment/FX charges.
- Net margin % = NAVARIS net fee / contractually defined fee base × 100; show the fee base explicitly.
- Freight unit cost = total quoted transport cost / chargeable units; specify kg/CBM/container/truckload and chargeable-weight rules.
- Expected value may be calculated only when probability assumptions are explicit: expected net fee = net fee × evidence-based close probability. Label it an estimate, never cash.
- DSO = average trade receivables / credit sales × days in period.
- DSCR = cash available for debt service / scheduled debt service; do not substitute total assets or gross revenue.
- OER definition must be fixed to the supplied dataset and consistently exclude/include depreciation as specified; flag non-comparable definitions. A threshold is a diagnostic trigger, not an automatic headcount action.
- Never apply a blanket 15% headcount reduction from OER alone; require role-level productivity, safety, contractual, service-level and labor-law analysis.

## 7. FDD prompt corrections
For distressed logistics companies, request at least 3 years audited statements, monthly trial balances, AR/AP aging, bank/debt schedules, fleet/warehouse utilization, shipment profitability, customer concentration, claims, customs delays, tax exposures and related-party transactions. Calculate liquidity, leverage, DSCR, DSO/DPO, cash conversion, gross margin by lane/customer, asset utilization and break-even volume only when inputs exist. Mark unavailable inputs N/A; never silently substitute zero. Recommend restructuring options only after cash-flow waterfall, creditor priority, legal jurisdiction and financing eligibility are checked.

3. Missing gates and reason the opportunity is not yet qualified.
4. Net-fee calculation with inputs, assumptions and downside case.

7. CASH COLLECTED with payment evidence reference; otherwise zero.
No invented emails, no bulk spam, no external send without specific approval.
