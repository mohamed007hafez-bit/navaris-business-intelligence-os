# restructuring

## Input Checklist
- Objective, scope, decision owner and deadline
- Source documents, dates, owner and verification status
- Baseline KPIs and reporting period
- Stakeholders, constraints, compliance requirements and risk appetite
- Written scope, data permission and confidentiality terms

## Template Excel Structure
Read Me & Scope; Raw Inputs (source/date/unit/evidence); Assumptions; Analysis and formulas; Risk Register; Action Plan (owner/date/KPI); Dashboard (baseline/target/actual/variance).

## Output PDF Structure
Cover/confidentiality; Executive Summary; Facts/source register; Calculations/assumptions; Findings/scenarios; Risks/mitigations/evidence gaps; 30/60/90-day action plan; Appendix.

## 3-minute Loom Script
0:00–0:20 problem/decision; 0:20–0:50 inputs and evidence gaps; 0:50–1:40 workbook and formulas; 1:40–2:20 findings and risks; 2:20–2:50 actions/owners; 2:50–3:00 decision and review date.

Quality gate: separate FACT, SOURCE, CALCULATION, ASSUMPTION, INTERPRETATION, RISK and ACTION. Never invent rates or count unverified cash. All outputs DRAFT until approved.
