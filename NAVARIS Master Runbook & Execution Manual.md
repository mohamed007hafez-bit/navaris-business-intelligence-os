## 3. CASH EXECUTION & STATE SEPARATION PROTOCOL
To prevent financial discrepancies, all cash items pass through rigorous state separation:
- `FORECAST`: Projected commission based on verified opportunity pipeline.
- `PENDING_VERIFICATION`: Bank receipt reference submitted but awaiting independent verification.
- `RECEIVED_VERIFIED`: Deposit confirmed against bank ledger records.
- `APPROVED`: Explicit manual sign-off executed by Dr. Mohamed Hafez.
- `REJECTED`: Invalid or unverified evidence.

*Default Rule:* `CASH COLLECTED = 0` until explicit human approval is logged.
