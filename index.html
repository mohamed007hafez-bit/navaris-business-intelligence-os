WHAT NAVARIS DOES

Practical support for commercial and operational challenges.

Scope is agreed against the client's evidence, objectives, available data and decision needs. Findings distinguish verified facts, assumptions and recommendations.

📊

Financial Statement Analysis

Review income statements, balance sheets and cash-flow statements; assess liquidity, leverage, margins, working capital, cash conversion and financial trends. Identify questions requiring management or auditor validation.

🧭

Business Diagnosis & Problem Solving

Map symptoms to root causes using process analysis, KPI review, cost drivers, bottleneck analysis and evidence-based prioritization. Deliver a practical action register with owners, measures and timing.

♻️

Restructuring & Turnaround

Assess operating performance, cost base, organizational roles, working capital and cash pressure; develop stabilization options, implementation milestones and monitoring indicators. Outcomes depend on data and management decisions.

⚙️

Operations & Process Development

Improve workflows, governance, service delivery, supplier coordination, project controls, KPIs and continuous improvement. Focus on measurable performance rather than activity alone.

💻

Digital Transformation

Assess digital readiness, process automation opportunities, data quality, integration, adoption and governance. Build a staged roadmap linked to business value, risk and implementation capacity.

📈

Investment & Commercial Assessment

Structure opportunity screening, market and counterparty checks, commercial assumptions, scenario analysis, cash-flow logic and risk registers. Investment decisions remain with the client and qualified advisers.

🛢️

Global Energy Intelligence

Decision support for energy and oilfield businesses through financial and operating-performance review, including maintenance reliability, downtime, utilization, cost drivers and performance indicators. Analysis depends on verified client data.

🚢

Freight & Commercial Representation

Commercial introductions and coordination across Egypt, the GCC and Asian trade routes. Support can include shipment requirements, partner qualification, route-cost comparison, quotation coordination and delivery follow-up. Freight rates and capacity are confirmed with providers before commitment.

💹

Investment Funds & Opportunity Assessment

Structured review of performance, risk, fees, manager quality, benchmark fit, consistency and investor suitability. Scenario analysis supports decisions; it does not replace regulated investment advice or guarantee returns.

📉

Cost Management & Profit Improvement

Identify direct, indirect and hidden costs; assess inflation exposure, waiting time, rework, demurrage and currency effects; prioritize measurable savings with owners, baselines and implementation checks.

🌐

Market Entry & Supplier Coordination

Support market and counterparty screening, supplier introductions, commercial follow-up and documented terms for cross-border business. Availability, regulatory compliance and any commission arrangement require confirmation before execution.

CORPORATE TRAINING
