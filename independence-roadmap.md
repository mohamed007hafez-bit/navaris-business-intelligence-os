# NAVARIS — 7-Day Operational Independence Roadmap

Seven days can establish a repeatable workflow; it cannot guarantee revenue or financial independence.

| Day | Deliverable | Acceptance |
|---|---|---|
| 1–2 | Finalize nine verticals and separate scopes | Nine READMEs; source-backed claims; commercial representation separate from shipping/NVOCC |
| 3–4 | Deploy AI Cash Engine and PDF update workflow | JSON validates; placeholder PDFs labeled; workflow passes; review changes before commit |
| 5–6 | Founder-approved LinkedIn + ten Borg prospect drafts | Ten qualified prospects with source log; approve drafts before sending |
| 7 | Daily 30-minute PDF/source routine | 10 min evidence review, 10 min playbook update, 10 min lead/follow-up log |

Cash loop: FACT → SOURCE → CALCULATION → ASSUMPTION → EVALUATION → ACTION. Validate like-for-like quotes, obtain founder approval, and record cash only with bank receipt proof.

**Done means:** documented SOPs, functioning deployments, source-backed lead tracker and founder can operate without developer help. Commercial independence requires measured results after launch.
